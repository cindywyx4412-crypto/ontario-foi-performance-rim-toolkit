# Ontario Ministry FOI Performance Review & Branch RIM Toolkit (2023–2025)

An independent practice project analyzing how high-volume Ontario ministries handle access requests under the *Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act* (FIPPA), with an illustrative records and information management (RIM) toolkit for a ministry branch.

**Author:** Yuxin (Cindy) Wang

## Deliverables

| File | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `Ontario_FOI_Performance_Brief.pdf` | 5-page policy brief: ministry timeliness, MECP spotlight, request outcomes, IPC appeals, July 1, 2026 FIPPA changes, recommendations with indicators |
| `Ontario_FOI_Performance_RIM_Toolkit.xlsx` | Excel workbook: source data, live calculations, charts, and the RIM toolkit |

## Key findings

1. **Volume is concentrated.** Provincial institutions received 31,042 FIPPA requests in 2025. The Ministry of the Environment, Conservation and Parks (MECP) and the Ministry of the Solicitor General together received 59% of them.
2. **MECP cleared a backlog, then held its ground.** MECP completed 12,620 requests in 2023 against 7,532 received. Its share of completed requests taking over 90 days fell from 47.2% (2023) to 11.1% (2024) and 12.3% (2025).
3. **Timeliness is slipping as volume grows.** Province-wide, the share of requests completed within 30 days fell from 78.1% (2024) to 71.2% (2025).
4. **One in five provincial outcomes is "no records".** "No responsive records exist" made up 22% of provincial outcomes in 2025, against 8% for municipalities.
5. **Process failures drive appeals.** Provincial appeals to the IPC rose 38% in 2025; 28% of all access appeals opened that year concerned a deemed refusal or an inadequate search.

## RIM toolkit (illustrative)

Designed for a hypothetical environmental approvals and compliance branch:

- **File plan:** 14 function-based record series across administration, finance, approvals, compliance, information requests and stakeholder relations
- **Retention crosswalk:** retention triggers, illustrative periods, final disposition and likely FIPPA considerations for each series
- **FOI workflow:** nine steps aligned with the FIPPA amendments in force from July 1, 2026 (45-business-day response time, staged access plans, fee estimates)
- **Training checklist:** eight modules covering the *Archives and Recordkeeping Act, 2006*, transitory records, FIPPA searches and privacy breaches
- **Search log:** a template for documenting each records search, supporting extension decisions, fee estimates and reasonable-search appeals

Retention periods are placeholders for design purposes only and must be confirmed against the applicable approved Ontario Public Service common or ministry records schedule.

## Method

- Eight ministries reported in all three years, plus the provincial total. The Ministry of Health is excluded because it does not appear in the 2024 FIPPA table.
- Every transcribed row was checked against the source PDFs, and recalculated rates match the IPC's reported rates within 1 percentage point.
- Requester, outcome and appeal figures cover all provincial institutions (including colleges, universities and hospitals) and are not broken down by ministry.
- Percentages are shares of completed requests. 2023–2025 performance is measured against the 30-calendar-day standard in force at the time.
